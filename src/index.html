01 / THE FOUNDATION

Academic structure

Organise academic years, grades, sections, and subjects. Give daily academic work a structure your team can recognise. Keep subjects and sections connected to the academic year they belong to.

02 / THE JOURNEY

Admissions workflows

Define admission stages and transitions. Validate and preview a workflow before publishing its version for use. Different intake journeys can use different published workflows, rather than sharing one fixed sequence.

03 / THE DETAILS

Forms

Build admission forms with sections, fields, validation, and visibility rules. Preview the experience before publishing. Collect the information each admission stage needs, with field rules defined in the form.

04 / THE RESPONSIBILITY

Roles and permissions

Set access through permissions and scope. Give people the tools and records relevant to their responsibilities. Permission checks govern actions; scope governs which records a person can see.
